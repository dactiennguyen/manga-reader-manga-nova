Description 3983/4000 ký tự
Manganova is a manga studio for your iPhone and iPad. Open a blank page and start drawing, split a page into panels, or plan your story first. Then letter it, read it like a real book and export it. There is no account to make and no subscription to manage, and your work stays on your device.

START THE WAY YOU LIKE
• Draw now: go straight to a blank page and sketch.
• Lay out a page: pick a panel template, then fill each panel.
• Write first: plan scenes and dialogue, then turn them into pages.
• Choose manga pages (B5 or A5, read right to left) or a vertical webtoon strip.
• Open the sample story "Lantern Courier" to see a finished chapter and take it apart.

DRAW WITH REAL TOOLS
• G-pen, pencil, brush, eraser, shapes, lasso fill and a colour picker.
• Screentone fills at 10, 25, 40 and 60% density.
• Save your own pens and tones in sets, and share a set as a small file.
• Up to 8 layers per panel with opacity and lock, and 50 undo steps.
• Two fingers zoom and rotate. Two-finger tap undoes, three-finger tap redoes.
• Stylus pressure, plus a stylus mode that keeps your palm off the page.
• Import a photo or sketch onto a layer and ink over it.

RULERS THAT KEEP YOUR LINES TRUE
• One-point and two-point perspective with vanishing points you can drag.
• Strokes settle on the line to the point, or on level and upright lines.
• Symmetry repeats every stroke across a line you can move.

MATERIALS AND REFERENCES
• Line-art backgrounds: classroom, street, rooftop, bedroom, forest path and city.
• Patterns and effects: sparkles, petals, rain, impact burst, clouds and leaves.
• Pin pictures on reference boards and float one beside the page while you draw.

KEEP YOUR CHARACTERS ON MODEL
• Pin a character on the canvas with their face and appearance notes.
• Profiles with role, traits, goal, voice, expressions and relationships.

LAY OUT YOUR PAGES
• 31 panel templates: grids, diagonal cuts, full bleeds and 4-koma strips.
• Drag panel borders, cut a panel with a line, merge panels and tilt corners.
• See every page on the storyboard, drag to reorder and track what is finished.

LETTER AND POLISH
• Speech, thought, shout, whisper and machine bubbles, narration boxes and SFX.
• 12 lettering fonts, from handwritten to heavy display. Drag, resize and rotate anything.
• Speed lines, focus lines, tones, gradients and sparkles.

PLAN YOUR STORY
• Outline acts and chapters on a drag-and-drop board.
• Write scene by scene, then turn a chapter into pages and panels in one tap.
• Keep a world wiki of places, factions, terms and events, with a timeline.
• Set a deadline for a chapter. The creation calendar splits the pages into a daily plan and reminds you the day before.

KEEP DRAWING EVERY DAY
• Short guided lessons: lines, eyes, faces, hands, perspective and manga effects.
• Creation statistics: strokes, drawing days, streaks, pages finished and words written, by week, month and year.
• A daily prompt, an optional daily reminder and Home Screen widgets for your streak.

PLAY TOGETHER NEARBY
Phones and iPads on the same Wi-Fi can draw on one page together, play a drawing relay, a speed battle or a guessing game, write a script together or send a whole story. No internet and no server are needed. The host can remove anyone, and you can block or report a person at any time.

READ AND SHARE
• Read your chapter like a real manga, with page spreads in landscape.
• Export PDF, PNG pages in a ZIP, CBZ for comic readers, or one long webtoon image.
• Back up a whole story as one .manganova file and open it on another device.

OPTIONAL WRITING HELP
Connect your own OpenAI-compatible server for help with the words: shape a story from an idea, plan chapters or find plot holes. It is off until you add a server, it helps with text only, and it never draws for you.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your stories, drawings and characters stay on your device. There are no ads, no analytics and no sign-in. Nothing is uploaded unless you set up a server yourself.

Notes 2451/4000 ký tự
Manganova is a manga drawing app that works fully offline. There is no account or sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases.

QUICK TOUR
1. On Home, tap "Open the sample story" to load "Lantern Courier", a finished 4-page chapter.
2. Open a page from the Storyboard. The Panels, Art and Lettering tabs at the bottom switch between layout, drawing and speech bubbles. Tap a panel in the Art tab to draw in it; the ruler tool has perspective guides and the menu has Materials.
3. The eye icon on the Storyboard opens the reader.

PLAY TOGETHER (user content, Guideline 1.2)
This needs two devices on the same Wi-Fi. Two simulators on the same Mac also find each other.
- Device A: Home > Play together, then tap "Create a room" under "Host a room". By default a room is joined only with its room code or QR code.
- Device B: Home > Play together. Tap the room under "Rooms on this Wi-Fi" and type the code shown on Device A, or type the 10-character room code under "Join with a room code".
- People in a room can only exchange drawings, short text, voice lines for dubbing, stories and brush sets with others in the same room. Nothing goes through a server, and nothing is public.
- Tap a person's name in the room's member list to open Remove from room (host only), Block and Report. A blocked person is refused automatically and cannot rejoin. Profile > Blocked and reported people lists blocks and reports and lets the user unblock.
- Reports are kept on the device and can be emailed to us at mangamarker@gmail.com.

AI WRITING HELP (optional, you can skip it)
AI is off until the user enters the address of their own OpenAI-compatible server in Profile. We do not run or provide a server. Without a server, the AI tools show sample answers that are clearly labelled "Sample answers" so the feature can be seen without one. AI only helps with text. The app never generates drawings with AI. The artwork in the sample story was imported into the app as image layers.

PERMISSIONS
- Local Network: only for Play together.
- Camera: only to scan a room's QR code or a friend's card in Play together.
- Microphone: only to record a voice line in Dub together.
- Notifications: only when the user turns on the daily drawing reminder in Profile.
- Photos: the system photo picker is used, so the app has no photo library permission.

WIDGET
Long-press the Home Screen, tap +, and add Manganova. It shows the drawing streak and today's prompt.
