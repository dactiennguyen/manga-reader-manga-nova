Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 November 2026. Applies to the Manganova app for iPhone, iPad and Android (app ID com.manganova.manga.reader.org).

The short version: what you make stays on your device. Manganova has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics, and nothing you make is sent to us. The app only connects to an AI server you set up yourself, and to friends' phones on the same Wi-Fi when you play together.

What is stored on your device

The app keeps the following in its private storage on your device:

  • Your stories: titles, outlines, scripts, characters, world notes, pages, panels, drawings, bubbles and effects.
  • Images you import, such as covers, character sheets, reference pictures and images on a layer, and the bundled materials you place in a panel.
  • Files you export (PDF, PNG, CBZ, long images and .manganova backups) until you delete or share them.
  • Your settings: pen name, theme, drawing hand, creative preferences and the AI server settings described below.
  • Conversations with the assistant, if you use it.

None of this is sent to us, and we have no access to it. Removing the app (or, on Android, clearing its storage in settings) deletes it. Stories you delete inside the app stay in the app's Trash for 30 days and are then removed for good.

No account

Manganova has no account or sign-in and does not ask for your name or email address. The optional pen name is stored on your device, shown in the app and printed on the cover and information pages of files you export.

Optional AI writing help

AI features stay off until you enter the address of a server in Profile. This must be a server you choose (an OpenAI-compatible endpoint). We do not run or provide one. Before the server is saved, the app shows what text is sent and asks you to allow sending it to that server; nothing is sent without your permission, and a new address asks again.

When you use an AI feature, the app sends the text that request needs to that server: for example your story's title and logline, character profiles, world notes marked to be sent, the relevant part of the script, and what you type. Nothing is sent until you tap an AI button. The app does not send your drawings or images.

The server you choose decides how that text is handled, so please read its privacy terms. An API key, if you enter one, is stored on your device and sent only to that server. Clearing the server address turns these features off.

Play together nearby

Play together connects phones on the same Wi-Fi directly, with no server in between. Only what you choose to send goes to the other phones in your room: your pen name, drawings, text, voice lines you record for dubbing, stories you send and brush sets you share. Each phone also sends a random code, made on your device, so that people you block stay blocked. Nothing from a room is sent to us.

You can remove someone from a room you host, block them or report them. Blocks and reports are kept on your device. A report is sent to the developer only if you choose to email it.

Permissions

Each permission is asked for only when you use the feature that needs it.

  • Photos and files: only the pictures or files you pick, through the system photo picker or file picker: an image to import, reference pictures, a .manganova or .mangaka file to restore, a brush file, or a place to save an export. The app cannot see the rest of your photos or files.
  • Camera: only to scan a room's QR code or a friend's mangaka card inside the app. The camera image is read on your device to find the code and is not saved or sent.
  • Microphone: only while you record a voice line for a speech bubble in Dub together. The recording goes to the phones in your room when you send it, and nowhere else.
  • Local network and Wi-Fi: to find rooms and connect to friends' phones on the same Wi-Fi for Play together.
  • Internet: to reach the AI server you set up, and for the connections between phones in Play together.
  • Notifications: for the drawing reminder you can turn on in Profile, including today's page plan and a notice the day before a chapter deadline. They are made on your device; nothing is sent to a server.
  • Run at startup (Android): only to set the reminder again after the phone restarts.
  • Vibration: for haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Profile.

The app does not use your location, contacts or an advertising identifier.

Analytics, advertising and third parties

The app contains no analytics, crash reporting or advertising SDKs, and we do not share data with third parties. The only outside party that can receive anything is the AI server you set up yourself. The App Store and Google Play may collect data of their own when you download the app; that is covered by Apple's and Google's privacy policies. This website stores no cookies of its own; its fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

Sharing and exports

When you share an exported file through the system share sheet, it goes to the app you pick, such as email or a messaging app. That app's privacy policy applies from then on.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not collect personal information from anyone, so there is nothing held by us to delete. Removing the app removes all local data.

Changes to this policy

If the way the app handles data changes, we will update this page and the date at the top, and note important changes in the release notes.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or a report about someone you met in Play together? Email mangamarker@gmail.com. More help is on the support page.
