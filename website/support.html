Manganova works without an account, and everything you make stays on your device. Most answers are below. For anything else, write to mangamarker@gmail.com.

Contact

Email mangamarker@gmail.com. To help us help you, include your device model, its system version and the app version (Profile, then About), and what you were doing when the problem happened. A screenshot helps a lot.

Report someone or something

In Play together, tap a person's name in the room to remove them (if you host), block them or report them. Choose "Also send to the developer" in the report form to email it to us. Every AI answer also has a report button.

We review reports within 24 hours. Blocked people cannot join rooms you host, and you are told when you are in a room with someone you blocked. You can see and undo blocks in Profile, under "Blocked and reported people".

Where your stories are kept

Only on your device, in the app's own storage. Nothing is uploaded to us, so we cannot see or restore your work. Deleted stories stay in the Trash (Profile, then Trash) for 30 days.

Back up or move to a new device

Open a story, tap the menu and choose Export, then pick the .manganova backup. Save it to Files, a cloud drive or send it to yourself. On the new device, open that file with Manganova to import the story. You can also send a story straight to a friend's phone on the same Wi-Fi with Play together, then "Send a story".

Play together does not find a room

  • Both devices must be on the same Wi-Fi network. Guest or public Wi-Fi often blocks devices from seeing each other; a home network or a phone hotspot works better.
  • On iPhone and iPad, allow Local Network for Manganova in Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Local Network.
  • If the room still does not show, the host can show its QR code and the guest can scan it from the Play together screen, or type the room code.
  • Turn off VPN apps on both devices while you play.

Reminders do not show

Turn the reminder on in Profile and allow notifications when asked. If you said no before, allow them in your device settings under Notifications, then Manganova. On Android, battery saver can delay reminders by a few minutes.

AI writing help

AI features help with words only, never with drawing, and stay off until you enter the address of an AI server you choose in Profile. We do not run a server. If AI answers fail, check the address, the API key and that the server is reachable from your device.

Delete your data

Delete stories inside the app and empty the Trash, or remove the app to delete everything it stored. We hold no data about you, so there is nothing to delete on our side. See the privacy policy.

Supported devices

iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 16 or later, and Android 7.0 or later. A stylus such as Apple Pencil works and is recommended for drawing.
